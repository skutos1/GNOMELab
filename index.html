Postdoctoral Fellows

Dr. Corinne Vietorisz | she/her

Corinne is a postdoctoral researcher in the lab whose work examines the molecular mechanisms fire-adapted fungi use to restore soil hydrological and biogeochemical properties after wildfire. She is excited about working with land managers to test how soil inoculations can help improve soil recovery and tree survival after severe wildfire.

In her free time, Corinne loves to ski, run, paint surreal landscapes and creatures, and check out local live music (any Seattle music recommendations are welcome!).

Graduate Students

Andrea Muñoz Ledo Farré (2025-present) | she/her

Andrea is a PhD student in the lab, co-advised by Julie Larson (Plant Regeneration Lab), broadly interested in plant-soil feedbacks. Her research explores the combined effect of invasive grass encroachment and severe wildfire on soil microbial communities, and the resulting impact on native plant regeneration, competition-coexistence dynamics, and thus plant community composition. Her work is based in water-limited ecosystems (primarily grassland-forest ecotones) across the Pacific West.

Andrea is also the Associate Program Director of the UW Campus Sustainability Fund. Outside of the UW, she is a dancer, runner, baker, painter, admirer of trees and mushrooms, and loves walking around Seattle parks.

Joey Mann (2026-present) | he/him

Joey (he/him) is a PhD student in the lab whose research is focused on using experimental evolution and natural variation to understand how and if arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi will adapt to stresses brought on by climate change. He is co-advised by Dr. Maitreya Dunham in the Genome Sciences Department, and hopes to develop useful genomic resources for mycorrhizal fungal studies.
